How to Descale a Steam Iron

Limescale builds up inside a steam iron over time and can clog the steam vents. Descaling every one to three months keeps it working well.

What you need
White vinegar or a descaling solution, distilled water and a soft cloth.

Step by step
Fill the tank with a half and half mix of vinegar and water. Heat the iron, hold it over a sink and press the steam burst until the tank is empty. Rinse with clean water and wipe the soleplate.

Prevent limescale
Use distilled water if your tap water is hard. Empty the tank after each use.
