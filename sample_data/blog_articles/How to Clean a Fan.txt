How to Clean a Fan

A dusty fan moves less air and can spread dust around the room. Cleaning it a few times a year takes about ten minutes.

Before you start
Unplug the fan. Remove the front grille if it clips off.

Cleaning the blades
Wipe each blade with a damp cloth, then dry it. Use a soft brush for the grille and motor housing.

Keep it running well
A quick dusting every month stops build-up and keeps the fan quiet.
