Best Cordless Vacuum Cleaners: A Buyers Guide

Looking for the best cordless vacuum cleaner? This guide covers what matters most before you buy, so you can pick a model that fits your home.

Suction power
Suction is measured in air watts. Most homes are well served by 100 to 150 air watts on hard floors, while thick carpet and pet hair benefit from more.

Battery runtime
Runtime ranges from 20 to 60 minutes depending on the mode you use. Boost mode drains the battery much faster than standard cleaning.

Filtration and dustbin size
A sealed HEPA filter keeps fine dust inside the machine. A larger dustbin means fewer trips to the bin, but it also adds weight.
